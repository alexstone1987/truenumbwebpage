Truenumb is a trading name of Fern Consulting Limited (company number 13903834), registered at 128 City Road, London, United Kingdom, EC1V 2NX. We are committed to protecting your personal data and handling it in accordance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. What data we collect

We collect the following personal data when you create an account or use our service:

  • Name and email address

  • Company name and role

2. How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain the truenumb service

  • To process subscription payments via Stripe
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Payment data

All payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not store or process card details on our servers. Stripe operates under their own PCI-compliant infrastructure. You can review Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/gb/privacy.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure within the United Kingdom. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. Access to data is controlled by role-based permissions within the platform.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days unless we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory reasons.

6. Sharing your data

We do not sell your data. We only share your data with third parties where necessary to operate the service:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Microsoft Azure — cloud hosting and data storage

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@truenumb.com.

8. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies required for the platform to function (such as session authentication tokens). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a notice within the platform.
