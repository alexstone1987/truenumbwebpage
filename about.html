About truenumb

The single source of truth for your business numbers.

Why we built truenumb

Every business runs on numbers — revenue, costs, headcount, KPIs. But for most companies, those numbers live in dozens of different spreadsheets, inboxes and systems, with different versions circulating to different people. By the time a number reaches the board or an investor, nobody is quite sure which version is correct or who signed it off.

truenumb was built to solve that problem. We wanted to give businesses a single, structured place to enter, review, approve and share their official data — with a complete audit trail and clear ownership at every step.

The result is a platform where management, boards, investors and lenders can all refer to the same trusted set of numbers, knowing exactly who put them there and who approved them.

What we do

truenumb is a cloud-based data management platform for businesses that take their reporting seriously. Teams use truenumb to:

  • Create structured datasets for KPIs, financials and operational metrics
  • Assign data entry and review responsibilities to the right people
  • Review and approve numbers before they are shared externally
  • Lock reporting periods once data is finalised
  • Share clean, approved datasets with investors, lenders and the board
  • Maintain a complete audit trail of every change

Data can be imported from Excel workbooks or connected directly from other systems using our API. Everything is available for export at any time.

Who we work with

truenumb is used by CFOs, Finance Directors, operations, investors, lenders and their teams who are responsible for producing reliable business data. Our customers need confidence that the numbers they share are accurate, approved and traceable.
